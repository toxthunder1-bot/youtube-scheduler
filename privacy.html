Privacy Policy: YouTube Scheduler

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Who uses it

YouTube Scheduler is a personal tool. Only its owner signs in to it, with the owner's own Google accounts, to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. No one else's account is used.

What it accesses

  • YouTube Data API (scope youtube.force-ssl): uploads the owner's videos, sets their titles, descriptions, tags, captions and thumbnails, and reads the owner's videos and the public comments on them.
  • YouTube Analytics API (scope yt-analytics.readonly): reads the owner's channel and video statistics.

Where the data goes

  • Everything stays on the owner's own computer. The Google sign-in is kept in the computer's protected credential store.
  • Channel statistics and comment text (with links, @handles and email addresses removed, and no commenter names) are summarised once a day by Anthropic's Claude to help the owner plan future videos.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or given to anyone else.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

By using this tool you are agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data can be deleted by deleting the tool's files from the owner's computer.
